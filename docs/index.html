HOW TO READ THIS

What these numbers do — and do not — show

They show a reproducible gain on some verifiable problems when Nexus has an appropriate executor or verifier.

They do not show universal improvement in reasoning, creativity, code or open-domain knowledge.

Publication rule: previously observed answers are never reused to score a new campaign; sealed sets are not used for training.
